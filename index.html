About

My name is Satyajit — most people call me Chris . I recently graduated from Rutgers University–New Brunswick with a B.S. in Astrophysics and a minor in Mathematics , where my research focused on applying machine learning to gravitational lens modeling. I'm now looking for entry-level roles in astrophysics research and data science, where I can put data-driven methods to work on hard problems.
